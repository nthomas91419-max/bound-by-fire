TRIAL I

THE CROSSING

◆

The wind hits first.

It tears across the mountain pass hard enough to steal the breath from your lungs.

Ahead, a narrow stone crossing stretches across a gorge so deep that the bottom disappears into darkness.

One by one, the riders step onto the ancient stone.

No railings. No ropes. No second chances.

You are halfway across when a scream cuts through the wind.

A rider behind you has slipped.

One hand clings desperately to the edge.

The instructors do not move.

They are watching.

What do you do?
Somewhere beyond the clouds, wings shift against the wind.

TRIAL II

THE COMMAND

◆

The training yard smells of steel, sweat, and smoke.

Across from you stands another candidate, weapon raised.

The command is simple.

Fight.

Steel crashes against steel.

Your opponent is fast, but something is wrong.

They favor one leg. Their breathing is uneven. Beneath the leather at their side, a dark stain is spreading.

They are injured.

You hesitate.

The instructor notices.

His voice cuts across the yard.

"Finish it."

WHAT DO YOU DO?
Somewhere beyond the walls, something ancient listens.

TRIAL III

THE ENCOUNTER

◆

The Academy told you the dragons were kept beyond the eastern wall.

They lied.

Something enormous is standing in the corridor ahead of you.

Black-gold scales scrape the stone. Smoke curls from its nostrils. One molten eye fixes on you.

There is no instructor. No weapon in your hand. No obvious way around it.

The dragon lowers its head until its breath warms your face.

WHAT DO YOU DO?
The dragon's pupil narrows.

A VOICE IN THE HALL

PROFESSOR VAELOR

◆

“Rider.”

Professor Vaelor steps from the shadows, silver at his temples catching the firelight.

“There is a difference between contradiction and uncertainty. The first is human. The second becomes dangerous when you refuse to examine it.”

His gaze settles on you.

“A dragon does not require you to be predictable. It requires you to be true. Choose as the person you are—not as the person you think each trial wishes you to become.”

A faint smile touches his face.

“The trials are not searching for correct answers. Neither are the dragons.”

TRIAL V

THE FIRE

◆

The final trial begins with bells.

Then the western tower explodes.

Flame pours across the courtyard. Riders scatter. Instructors shout conflicting orders.

Through the smoke you see three things at once: a trapped first-year beneath a fallen beam, the gate controls that could contain the fire, and the path to the Choosing chamber standing open.

No one tells you what this trial is testing.

Perhaps this time, it is not a trial at all.

WHAT DO YOU DO?
Above the flames, dragons begin to roar.
