TRIAL I

THE CROSSING

◆

The wind hits first.

It tears across the mountain pass hard enough to steal the breath from your lungs.

Ahead, a narrow stone crossing stretches across a gorge so deep that the bottom disappears into darkness.

One by one, the riders step onto the ancient stone.

No railings. No ropes. No second chances.

You are halfway across when a scream cuts through the wind.

A rider behind you has slipped.

One hand clings desperately to the edge.

The instructors do not move.

They are watching.

What do you do?
Somewhere beyond the clouds, wings shift against the wind.

TRIAL II

THE COMMAND

◆

The training yard smells of steel, sweat, and smoke.

Across from you stands another candidate, weapon raised.

The command is simple.

Fight.

Steel crashes against steel.

Your opponent is fast, but something is wrong.

They favor one leg. Their breathing is uneven. Beneath the leather at their side, a dark stain is spreading.

They are injured.

You hesitate.

The instructor notices.

His voice cuts across the yard.

"Finish it."

WHAT DO YOU DO?
Somewhere beyond the walls, something ancient listens.
